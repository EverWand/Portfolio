Preproduction

3/18/2025

Overview


Starting out was a bit rough at the beginning as I had pivoted my original idea for SIP to SideQuest, making it so I had to come up with the concept and get it approved during the time I was meant to be in the production phase for the prototype. However, Pre-production is important nonetheless and I made sure to take the time to plan out a bit of what I wanted before stepping into creating a prototype. I utilized Figma in order to design wireframes of the User Interface for the different panels. I also used this as an opportunity to plan out the structure of how users can navigate through the different panels, The layout of elements, and to play with possible features to add for the project.
